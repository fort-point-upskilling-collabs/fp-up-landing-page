# Code of Conduct

Fort Point Upskilling Collaborations exists to make serious learning and useful collaboration more accessible.

Participants are expected to:

- be patient with differing experience levels;
- criticize ideas and work without belittling people;
- ask questions and explain decisions in good faith;
- give accurate credit;
- respect privacy, consent, and project boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and retaliation; and
- help repair mistakes when they occur.

## Reporting a concern

For ordinary project disagreements, speak with a project maintainer or organization owner. For sensitive conduct, privacy, or security concerns, contact an owner privately or email **fp.up.4th@gmail.com** rather than opening a public issue.

Organization owners may remove content, restrict access, or remove a participant when necessary to protect the community or its work.
