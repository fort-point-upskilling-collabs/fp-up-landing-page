# Fort Point Upskilling Collaboration Project Catalog — Short Edition

This is the fast-browsing version of the catalog: one compact description per project, followed by an abbreviation and specialized-term guide. The companion Long Edition adds repository locations, project status, fuller descriptions, and likely tools.

## Project Menu

### Pocket Cocktail Party
Separate understandable synthetic audio mixtures with ICA before moving to messier real recordings.

### Bilingual Boundary Lab
Use *War and Peace* to study how models learn boundaries between Russian and embedded French.

### English Gorf
Test whether English's Germanic and French histories leave detectable differences in letter-frequency patterns.

### Envelope ZIP Reading
Build inspectable MNIST digit recognizers and study individual mistakes, not only aggregate accuracy.

### JabberVec-l2v (X2vec family)
Explore the geometry learned by character embeddings trained on English-like text.

### Latent Forge
Develop a small recommender-system lab around baselines, matrix factorization, and holdout MSE.

### Text Cleanroom
Find and repair messy text and filename encodings through deterministic, auditable transformations.

### Multimodal Jupy Logger
Capture Jupyter/IPython work as durable multimodal logs for replay, rendering, and provenance.

### Mendeleev Spelling Bee
Represent multilingual words as sequences of chemical-element symbols and other symbol systems.

### Start Pirate Now
Learn R—or another compact tool—systematically from its canonical help and command-line documentation.

### Double-P Double-N
Create physically inspectable paper-and-pencil neural networks for teaching and research questions.

### Discrete Fourier Transforms and Sampling
Teach Fourier analysis and sampling through small, reproducible scientific-computing exercises.

### Higgs Boson Visualized
Turn Higgs-boson concepts into reproducible quantitative physics visualizations.

### RWKV + LoRA Learning Experiment
Reconstruct and diagnose an ambiguous LoRA fine-tuning experiment on RWKV.

### Job Application Word Frequency
Compare job descriptions and application materials with lightweight word-frequency analysis.

### Cyrillic-through-Latin OCR
Build paired data that can help reverse Latin-biased OCR corruption of Cyrillic text.

### Multilingual Name Corruption Lab
Generate historically plausible multilingual variants and errors for family-name searching.

### DIAL / Digital Sundial Learning
Learn a digital-sundial mapping from a tiny binary problem toward finer time and geometry.

### MorphoGender (X2vec family)
Measure how predictable grammatical gender is from the written form of Russian and French nouns.

### Syntactic Case Marking and Adpositions (X2vec family)
Predict adpositions or inflections that mark a noun phrase's relationship to a sentence.

### Ambiguity Corpus
Build and classify a corpus of syntactic ambiguity, puns, clarification phrases, and minimal examples.

### Measuring Instruction Inconsistency
Develop quantitative tests for conflicts within instructions or requirements, especially for LLM tasks.

### Twain/Feynman Model Collapse
Begin with playful style transformations, then study repeated training on model-generated text.

### Mechanics: Lagrange / Hamilton / T−V Simulations
Compare formulations of classical mechanics through small simulations that expose their relationships.

### RLHF Incomplete Ontology
Study preference-learning tasks whose required labels cannot represent every relevant case.

### Design by Inverse Angular Light
Reason backward from desired angular light behavior to a physical optical configuration.

### Info Theory for Pareidolia/Apophenia
Measure text-like or manuscript-like structure without depending on OCR or HTR results.

### NLP-Oriented Symbolic Encodings for Visual Structure
Encode spatial or visual relationships so language-model methods can operate on them.

### Symbolized Persistence Under Quantized Projection
Study which symbolic or structural information survives lower-detail representations.

### Underground Systems Prediction Dataset
Build a worldwide transit-system dataset and test which engineering and historical outcomes are predictable.

## Author-Attribution Project Family

These projects ask which aspects of writing style survive changes in time, language, translation, model, or prompt.

### Style Trajectories
Test which stylistic signals remain stable—or drift—between an author's early and late work.

### Translated-Author Attribution Experiment
Measure which author-identification signals survive translation within and across languages.

### LLM Author Attribution Analysis
Compare stylistic diversity across language models, configurations, prompts, and repeated runs.

### Relating Hip Hop/Rap to Germanic and Skaldic Poetry (X2vec family)
Compare sound patterns discovered in hip-hop with Germanic and especially skaldic poetry.

## What Ties These Projects Together

These collaborations favor interpretable, mathematically grounded work over black-box demonstrations. Many connect machine learning with language, historical documents, scientific computing, computer vision, research infrastructure, or careful dataset construction.

The catalog deliberately spans maturity levels: some projects are small learning exercises, some are collaboration-ready research seeds, and some support active publication work. A good first choice is not necessarily the grandest idea; it is the project whose next useful step is clear, testable, and interesting to the people doing it.
